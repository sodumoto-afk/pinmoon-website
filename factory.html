Located in Foshan, Guangdong — China's furniture capital — Pinmoon is a professional mattress manufacturer integrating R&D, production, and sales. Our 10,000m² facility houses complete in-house production lines covering every step from raw materials to finished products.

位于中国家具之都——广东佛山，Pinmoon 是一家集研发、生产、销售于一体的专业床垫制造企业。10,000m² 厂房配备完整自有产线，覆盖从原材料到成品的每一道工序。
